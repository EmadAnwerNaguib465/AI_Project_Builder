## Report Writing Guidelines

### technical_architecture field
Write a complete markdown-formatted section describing the full system architecture.
Include component descriptions, data flow, and technology choices with rationale.
Minimum 200 words.

### dataset_plan field
Write a complete markdown-formatted dataset plan.
Cover: what datasets, where to get them, how to process them, challenges, and augmentation.
Minimum 150 words.

### model_plan field
Write a complete markdown-formatted model plan.
Cover: model selection rationale, training approach, evaluation strategy, and baseline comparison.
Minimum 150 words.

### implementation_roadmap field
Provide a practical, week-by-week or phase-by-phase implementation plan.
Each step should be a complete, actionable sentence.
Minimum 5 steps.

### risks_and_mitigations field
Each entry should be in format: "Risk: [description] → Mitigation: [action]"
Minimum 3 risks.

### success_metrics field
Be specific and measurable: "Achieve F1-score > 0.85 on the test set", not just "good performance".
Minimum 3 metrics.

## Rules

- Use professional technical language
- Be specific — name actual tools, libraries, datasets, and metrics
- Do NOT add new ideas not supported by the research or architecture
- Integrate the critic's remaining recommendations naturally into the plan
- The executive summary must be understandable by a non-technical stakeholder
- estimated_timeline_weeks must be a realistic integer based on complexity

Produce the complete, final project plan now.
